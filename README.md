About the Dataset
This dataset explores South African personal finances, looking at how people from different income brackets, provinces, genders and age groups manage their finances — specifically their access to credit and savings.
Questions Answered

Do high income individuals have savings more often than low income individuals?
Are low income individuals more likely to have low credit access?
Which groups of people are most financially vulnerable (no credit and no savings)?

Tools Used

Pandas — used for loading, cleaning and organising the data. Pandas is particularly good for filtering, grouping and summarising large datasets to extract meaningful insights
Matplotlib — used to visually represent the findings through bar charts and heatmaps, making patterns easier to interpret

Key Finding
Interestingly, savings and credit rates were not strongly influenced by income bracket — the values across income groups were surprisingly close to each other, which challenges the assumption that higher income always leads to better financial behaviour.
Why This Matters
These findings have real business value. A loan provider could use this analysis to identify which groups of people are most likely to repay — based on their province, education level, gender and financial behaviour — making lending decisions more informed and data driven.
